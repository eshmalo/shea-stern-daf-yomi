# How to update the site — a guide for the Rov

Everything is done from one page: **https://monseydafyomi.com/admin/**
Sign in with the admin password (Elazar has it). You stay signed in for 12
hours on that device.

## Put up a worksheet / source sheet

1. Pick the page at the top — **Daf** (choose masechta + daf), **Parsha**, or
   **Yom Tov**.
2. Press **＋ Add a worksheet / source sheet** and choose the file
   (PDF, picture, or Word/PowerPoint).
3. Give it the title people should see (Hebrew is fine), press
   **Upload worksheet**.

It appears on that page of the site under **“Worksheets & sources”** within a
minute — visitors can open it or download it. The pencil renames it, the
arrows re-order, the ✕ removes it.

## Replace the audio or video of a shiur

1. Pick the page the same way.
2. Under **Shiur recording**, press **Upload** (or **Replace**) next to Audio
   or Video and choose the file.
3. Wait for the bar to finish — a large video can take a while; keep the page
   open until it says done.

Your recording immediately becomes what plays on that page — it outranks the
TorahAnytime copy. **Remove** puts the page back to the regular shiur. This
also works for a daf that has no shiur yet, and for parsha/yom tov pages
(the recording appears at the top of the page).

## Good to know

- Nothing is ever lost: every change is kept in history, and removing your
  upload just restores what was there before.
- If you get signed out, just sign in again with the same password.
- If something looks stuck, reload the page and try again — and if it still
  misbehaves, tell Elazar what page you were on and what you pressed.
